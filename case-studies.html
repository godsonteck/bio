Project 01 · Hotel Website

NS Luxury Villa Hotel Website

I work at this hotel's reception desk, and I built its website. This is what was breaking, what I built, and what changed at the front desk.

Open nsvilla.com ↗
The Problem

A phone that wouldn't stop ringing

The hotel had no website. Guests could not see the rooms or the prices for themselves, so every question landed on the front desk, the same few answers, all day long.

What I Made

A light, fast website

Photos of every room, prices in plain view, Google Maps directions, and one-tap buttons to call or message on WhatsApp. It opens quickly even on slow mobile data.

What Changed

Guests arrive informed

The simple calls slowed to a trickle. Guests arrive already knowing the rooms, the prices, and how to reach the team, and most confirm their stay on WhatsApp.

Project 02 · Online Store

CR Cosmetics & Essentials

How a beauty and skincare shop moved from WhatsApp status posts to a proper online store with nationwide delivery, and what changed once the orders started flowing.

Open crcosmeticsgh.com ↗
The Problem

Selling through WhatsApp statuses

Is this item in stock? How much with delivery? Every sale started with the same long back-and-forth, typed out again and again.

What I Made

A store built for phones

Products sorted into groups, clear descriptions, prices you don't have to ask about, and a simple checkout that works on the network most customers actually have.

What Changed

Orders that arrive complete

Every order now arrives with the items and the address already written down, and new customers keep finding the shop through Google, beyond the usual audience.
