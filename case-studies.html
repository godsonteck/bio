Case Study 01 · Hospitality

NS Luxury Villa Web Portal

How building a dedicated website for the boutique villa where I work as a receptionist eliminated phone call bottlenecks and boosted direct guest bookings.

Visit Live Site ↗
The Friction

Front Desk Overload

With no online presence, out-of-town travelers had no way to view rooms or verify rates. Our desk phone was constantly tied up answering basic repetitive questions.

The Build

Fast Mobile Booking Portal

I designed a clean, lightweight portal showcasing photo galleries for every room tier, clear pricing, Google Maps directions, and 1-tap WhatsApp and phone reservation buttons.

The Outcome

Guests Arrive Informed

Room inquiries dropped significantly at the front desk. Guests now arrive already familiar with room tiers, pricing, and amenities — and reach the team directly via WhatsApp or phone to confirm their stay.

Case Study 02 · E-Commerce

CR Cosmetics & Essentials

Moving a growing beauty and skincare retailer from scattered WhatsApp statuses to an automated online store with nationwide delivery across Ghana.

Visit Live Store ↗
The Friction

Messy DM Selling

Selling through WhatsApp status updates caused constant confusion over which items were in stock, prices, and shipping fees. Hours were wasted typing out details manually.

The Build

Mobile-First Storefront

Built a streamlined storefront with category filtering, clear product descriptions, structured pricing, and a smooth checkout flow designed for Ghanaian mobile connections.

The Outcome

Nationwide Trust & Sales

Orders now come in structured with delivery addresses and item selections clearly enumerated. Google search visibility also brings new customers outside the Volta Region.
