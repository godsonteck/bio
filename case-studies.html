ALERA Clinical Platform

A real product running under the SAD mindset: the dream had to survive contact with real clinical workflows.

Built to solve the pain of manual patient records and slow pharmacy communication. I created a system for clinics, labs, and pharmacies that reduces wait time and keeps sensitive records secure.

  • Challenge: fragmented medical workflows and missing patient data
  • Approach: full-stack portal with React, FastAPI, and PostgreSQL
  • Result: a unified clinical experience designed for Ghanaian health teams
