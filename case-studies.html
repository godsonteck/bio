01 / HOSPITALITY PLATFORM

NS Luxury Villa

Transitioning premier boutique hospitality in Ho from chaotic phone-call desk inquiries to a streamlined web booking portal.

0% OTA Fees Lost
<1s Load Time
24/7 Direct Availability
The Bottleneck

Zero online visibility & front desk overload

As a receptionist at NS Luxury Villa, I experienced the operational friction firsthand: travelers searched online for quality lodging in Ho, but the villa had no digital portal. Front-desk phone lines were constantly tied up answering routine questions regarding room tiers, amenities, pricing, and directions.

The Engineering Approach

Clean visual architecture & direct conversion triggers

I designed and deployed a high-speed web portal optimized for mobile visitors. The platform highlights room galleries, explicit amenities, transparent rates, and direct WhatsApp / phone booking flows that connect prospective guests directly with reception in one tap.

The Outcome

Autonomous guest inquiries & verified brand presence

Guests now browse room types and check policies independently prior to calling. The villa established high organic local search credibility, and direct reservations bypassed third-party booking commissions completely.

02 / E-COMMERCE SYSTEM

CR Cosmetics & Essentials

Engineering a dedicated digital storefront for an authentic beauty brand with inventory cataloging, mobile shopping, and nationwide logistics.

100% Product Catalog
Ghana Nationwide Shipping
Live Store Deployed
The Bottleneck

Fragmented DM selling & inventory confusion

Selling strictly through Instagram and WhatsApp status updates created constant customer confusion over pricing, stock availability, and shipping costs. The business owner spent hours typing out product details, confirming payments manually, and fielding stock inquiries.

The Engineering Approach

Structured product taxonomies & fast checkout flow

Developed and published a dedicated e-commerce catalog at crcosmeticsgh.com featuring category filtering, clear skincare specifications, transparent pricing, and a smooth checkout path designed specifically for mobile data networks in Ghana.

The Outcome

Credible brand perception & nationwide order fulfillment

Orders now arrive structured with delivery addresses and item selections clearly enumerated. The business gained national discoverability on Google search and established buyer confidence for customers outside the Volta Region.
