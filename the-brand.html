HO · GHANA Swipe
SAD / 01 · ACT

Choose to act.

A dream is a wish until you move. Success isn't luck or timing. It's the decision you make today, small or not.

SAD / 02 · PERMISSION

Give yourself permission.

Don't wait for someone to approve of you before you begin. Nobody is coming to hand you the green light, so you give it to yourself.

SAD / 03 · DISCIPLINE

The work nobody sees.

The unglamorous kind of work, done daily, with no audience. Applause comes later, if it comes at all.

SAD / 04 · ROOTS

Stay rooted.

I'm Ewe, from Dzodze, and I carry that wherever I go. Ambition that forgets where it comes from has nothing to stand on.

“Xevi dze atizigba dzi la, meŋlɔa anyigba be o.”

An Ewe proverb from home: Even when a bird perches high on the tallest tree, it never forgets the ground it came from.

SAD / 05 · RESILIENCE

Resilience.

Hard seasons don't cancel the dream. They test how much you meant it.
