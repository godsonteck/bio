# Personal vs brand separation

## Brand site reviewed

Source: https://successabovedreams.vercel.app/

Success Above Dreams already owns the business-facing positioning: websites, online stores, POS and business software; the company story; practice and process; selected work; solutions; insights; and project enquiries.

## Personal site direction

The personal Emmanuel Drah site should focus on Emmanuel himself: background, taste, working habits, current interests, selected work as personal evidence, photographs, milestones, learning, and direct human contact.

Avoid repeating brand language such as “practice,” service menus, project-scoping promises, five-step delivery process, and “discuss a project” as the main call to action.

Preferred personal navigation: Work, About, Now, Archive, Contact. Preferred calls to action: See what I’ve made, Read my story, Say hello, Open the archive.

The personal site may link to the Success Above Dreams brand site when the user wants company services, solutions, process, or a formal project enquiry.
