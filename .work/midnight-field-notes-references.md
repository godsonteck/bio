## Design decisions taken from the references

- Reduce the number of identical floating cards and let typography, curation, and real project imagery carry the experience.
- Use an editorial display contrast: oversized sans headlines with a serif italic emphasis.
- Introduce asymmetry through offset project columns, offset gallery images, and a vertical editorial rail.
- Keep deep blue as the foundation, with cobalt light and pale blue type as the accent system.
- Reserve neumorphism for controls and small interface objects instead of applying it to every content block.
- Use borders, rules, index markers, and generous whitespace to make the work feel selected rather than auto-generated.
- Keep motion restrained and functional: hover lift is limited to images and controls, while content remains calm.
