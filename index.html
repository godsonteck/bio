Why I do this work

I do not like building things that look impressive on paper and fall apart in real use. I prefer work that is useful, honest, and built around the people who will actually live with it.

I start with the friction

I usually begin with what is not working properly, what is slowing people down, and what a better system would make easier.

I keep it practical

Good work should feel clear, stable, and useful. I try to keep the technology simple enough that real people can trust it.

I build for the conditions on the ground

I think about weak networks, busy teams, and imperfect systems because that is where real work happens.
