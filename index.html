My Background

The journey behind the code

My perspective is shaped by the everyday realities of life and business in Ghana.

“I care about whether the software actually helps the person who has to open it every single morning.”

Growing up in Ghana, I noticed how often people had to work around broken or non-existent digital tools. After completing my degree in Information and Communication Technology at Ho Technical University, I spent my national service year at NHIS Dzodze managing public health records and member inquiries.

Working the reception desk at NS Luxury Villa taught me more about customer service and real workflow friction than any textbook. That is why I build systems that are easy to use, load fast on regular mobile connections, and solve actual problems.
