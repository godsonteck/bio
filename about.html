Emmanuel Drah
Ho, Volta Region, Ghana
Full-Stack Dev

“Working at the front desk of a villa taught me more about real human friction than any software tutorial ever could.”

By day, I work as a receptionist at NS Luxury Villa in Ho. When you spend your shifts checking guests into rooms, answering constant phone calls about rates, and sorting out everyday logistical details, you quickly see where the real bottlenecks are.

I built nsvilla.com not as a hypothetical student project, but because our front desk genuinely needed it. Today, guests browse the rooms on their phones and arrive with their questions already answered. That direct, practical connection to real-world problems is the same approach I bring to every website, e-commerce store, or tool I build.

Before this, I spent my national service year at NHIS Dzodze, managing member records in the health insurance database and supporting citizen inquiries. My technical education came from Ho Technical University, where I earned my Higher National Diploma in Information and Communication Technology.
