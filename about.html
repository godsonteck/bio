Background & Conviction

Rooted in Real Life

I am from Ghana, and I have always noticed the gap between how systems are supposed to work in theory and how they actually work in practice. That is where I begin.

By day, I work at the front desk of NS Luxury Villa in Ho, welcoming guests, managing room assignments, and handling daily operations. Observing guest inquiries and administrative bottlenecks in real time gives me direct operational insight that informs every line of code I write.

Under the Success Above Dreams studio, I build systems that hold up under real pressure: booking engines that eliminate front-desk chaos, e-commerce storefronts that automate order fulfillment across Ghana, and internal database tools for public service.
