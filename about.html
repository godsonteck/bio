About Me

I’m Emmanuel — someone who cares deeply about how people live, work, and grow.

I am from Ghana, and I have always been the kind of person who notices the small gaps between how things are supposed to work and how they actually work. That curiosity led me to build systems, but what matters most is that I build with people in mind. I want my work to feel usable, dependable, and connected to the real context where it will be used.

My journey

🌍

Childhood in Ghana

Curiosity came first

Growing up, I was always asking why things worked the way they did. I noticed how people adapted to imperfect systems, and I started to think about what would make those systems easier to use.

🎓

Learning how to build with purpose

My education taught me the technical skills, but more importantly it taught me how to listen to the problem before choosing the technology.

🤝

2023 – 2024 · NHIS Dzodze

How I stay grounded

My work is shaped by the people I meet and the places I see. I want the systems I build to work where they are used, not where they are imagined.

Look for real problems

I spend time with the actual workflow instead of assuming the ideal version of it.

Listen first

I pay attention to the people using the tool, and I shape the work around their needs.

Design for the environment

Tools should work where they are used — in busy offices, on weak networks, and in everyday routines.
