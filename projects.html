Project 01 · Healthcare Platform

ALERA Healthcare

Hospitals in Ghana were losing critical time because doctors, labs, and emergency teams couldn't share information efficiently. I built ALERA to fix that. It is a full-scale, real-time healthcare platform that connects every department — from patient intake and lab results to pharmacy inventory and ambulance dispatch — into a single unified dashboard. The result: faster decisions, better care.

Project 02 · Mobile Booking Engine

Rental Flow

For my final year project, I chose a problem I saw every day — renting a vehicle in Ghana requires too many calls, too much back-and-forth, and zero transparency. Rental Flow is a cross-platform mobile app that brings the entire process online: browse available cars, select a date range, confirm a booking, and track your rental — all in under three minutes. Built to feel as smooth as the car you're booking.
