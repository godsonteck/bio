System 01 · Production Standard

ALERA Clinical Platform

Our hospitals struggle with information sharing; patient data, pharmacy assets, and lab files get delayed across physical paper routes. I designed and built ALERA to unify medical roles into an instantaneous websocket-backed registry. Integrating doctors, labs, pharmacies, and dispatch services into one interface reduces latency where every second represents patient care.

System 02 · Completed Local Build Only

Rental Flow App

Renting a vehicle in our municipalities shouldn't involve endless speculative phone calls. I developed Rental Flow to bring fleet transparency directly to the smartphone. The app handles local device state caching, coordinates booking timelines, and matches users to fleets immediately. It represents my final year HTU project, completed and validated locally.
