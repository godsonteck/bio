Project 02 · Desktop Suite

SAD Desktop Suite

For many local organizations and businesses in Ghana, unstable internet shouldn't stop professional management. I am developing the SAD Desktop Suite—an offline-first set of desktop systems built with Electron. The suite provides tailored desktop applications, including BizCore (commercial sales and inventories), EduCore (school registries), MediCore (local clinical queues), and StayCore (hotel records).
