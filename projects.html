Healthcare Coordination

ALERA Healthcare

Hospitals frequently struggle with uncoordinated flows and slow report delivery. I built ALERA to connect critical medical pipelines in real-time. It enables doctors, lab technicians, pharmacies, and dispatch services to instantly update inventory, review patient profiles, and communicate smoothly.

Vehicle Booking Engine

Rental Flow

Vehicle renting in Ho relies on dozens of calls and complete lack of booking transparency. Rental Flow puts this entire dynamic on a clean, robust mobile engine. Built with Flutter, users can browse vehicles, track dates, confirm schedules, and secure rentals in under 3 minutes.
