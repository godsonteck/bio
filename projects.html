Live Hotel website nsvilla.com

NS Luxury Villa

Guests see rooms, prices, and directions before they call, and book on WhatsApp with the details already known.

Open nsvilla.com ↗
Client
NS Luxury Villa, a hotel in Lokoe, Ho
Role
Website design and build, end to end
Timeline
Built around my front-desk shifts, live and maintained since
Stack
Hand-built pages, Vite build, hosted on Vercel
Deliverables
Room and rate pages, photo gallery, Google Maps directions, WhatsApp and call buttons
Constraints
Guests on slow mobile data and older phones; prices the front desk must keep accurate

The problem

A phone that would not stop ringing. The hotel had no website. Guests could not see rooms or prices for themselves, so every question landed on the front desk: the same few answers, all day long.

What existed before

Word of mouth and WhatsApp statuses. Rooms, prices, and directions lived in conversations. Every enquiry started from zero, and nothing could be checked before calling.

What I changed

One place for everything a guest asks. Photos of every room, prices in plain view, Google Maps directions, and one-tap buttons to call or message on WhatsApp. It opens quickly even on slow mobile data.

Technical decisions

Information, not transactions. The hotel takes bookings on WhatsApp, so the site’s job is to inform, not to process payments. I kept it a light hand-built site with compressed images and no heavy booking platform, so it loads fast on the networks guests actually use.

What changed after launch

Guests arrive informed. The site put rooms, prices, directions, and WhatsApp contact in one place, so guests could answer many of their own pre-booking questions before calling the front desk. Booking conversations start with the details already known.

What I would improve next

An availability calendar. The next useful step is showing open dates per room, so guests can check availability before they message. That is the improvement I would make next.

Live Online store crcosmeticsgh.com

CR Cosmetics & Essentials

A catalogue people can search, with prices and a checkout that sends complete orders, instead of WhatsApp status posts alone.

Open crcosmeticsgh.com ↗
Client
CR Cosmetics & Essentials, a beauty and skincare shop
Role
Store design and build, end to end
Timeline
From first brief to live store, then ongoing updates
Stack
Custom JavaScript store: catalogue, cart, and customer accounts
Deliverables
Product categories, prices and offers, delivery and returns information, checkout that sends complete orders
Constraints
Customers ordering on phones over mobile data, with nationwide delivery

The problem

Selling through WhatsApp statuses. Is this in stock? How much with delivery? Every sale started with the same long back-and-forth, typed out again and again.

What existed before

Prices and stock lived in chat. Customers could only see what the latest status post showed. Nothing was searchable, and every order needed a conversation to complete.

What I changed

A store built for phones. Products sorted into groups with clear descriptions, prices you do not have to ask about, offers in one place, and a cart and checkout that work on the network most customers actually have.

Technical decisions

A custom store, not a rented platform. I built the catalogue, cart, and accounts as one light JavaScript app instead of stacking plugins on a heavy platform. Fewer moving parts means fewer things to break on a slow connection, and the shop keeps full control of its products and prices.

What changed after launch

Orders that arrive complete. The store gave the business a searchable product catalogue and a structured order flow instead of relying only on WhatsApp status posts. Orders arrive with the items and the delivery address already written down.

What I would improve next

Payment at checkout. Taking payment online in the same place the order is placed would close the last gap between browsing and buying. That is the next improvement I would build.
