Project 01 · Reliable Live Portal

ALERA Clinical Platform

This is the kind of work SAD stands for: a dream turned into a real healthcare system people can actually open and use.

Many hospitals and clinical facilities struggle to share patient files, pharmacy lists, and test results securely and in real time. I built ALERA as a full-stack health hub. It links doctors, laboratories, dispensaries, and emergency teams into a unified interface, drastically reducing wait times and securing private patient records.

Project 02 · Desktop Suite

SAD Desktop Suite

The product has to survive contact with real infrastructure, not just a clean pitch.

In meetings with local businesses, one problem kept coming up: software that breaks when the internet goes away. SAD Desktop Suite is my answer—a set of offline-first desktop tools designed to keep accounting, school records, clinic queues, and guest bookings working even when the network is unreliable.

DermScan AI

Prototypes can be inspiring, but the real test is whether the solution still feels useful when real people use it.

DermScan AI is a research project born from the need for faster skin care advice. I am building it to help users get quick, private feedback on potential skin issues before they decide whether to visit a clinic.
